Reading the Meeting Map

Wrap-Up listens to your meeting and draws it as a live map you can push back on. Skim the two figures, then spend your time in the sandbox — it is the real interface, and the checklist is done when you have tried every action.

The map

Each point the system hears is a node. Nodes sit in the group for the current topic, and groups stack down the page.

A group dims and takes a green tick once everything inside it is settled.

The four node types

Each one at rest, and the card that opens when you click it.

Dashed outlines mark what was added to the conversation rather than heard in it.

Try it

A working map with a made-up meeting in it. Click anything — nothing here is recorded, and you cannot break it.

Meetings move faster than anyone can keep track of.

Most of what goes wrong with a meeting goes wrong quietly. Nobody notices until after it’s over, when the people who could have fixed it have already left the room.

A good point gets lost.

Someone raises it, the conversation moves on before anyone answers, and by the end nobody remembers it was ever left open.

One topic eats the hour.

Nobody can see how long the room has been on it, or how much else is still waiting, until the meeting runs out of time.

The recap arrives too late.

One person’s notes become the record. If they heard it differently than you did, the moment to say so has already passed.

Wrap-Up puts the record in front of the room while the meeting is still running, so it can be checked, corrected and finished before everyone leaves.

Made for the corner of your eye.

In a meeting, your attention belongs to the people talking. Taking notes pulls you out of the conversation, and so does any tool you have to read closely. Wrap-Up is designed to sit at the edge of your attention: you keep listening, and a glance is enough to know where things stand.

A glance, not a read

Colour says whether a point is settled or open. Size says how much it matters. The halo shows where the room is right now. You can take it in without reading a single word.

Nobody has to take notes

Wrap-Up does the capturing, so no one spends the meeting typing. Everyone stays in the discussion, including the person who usually writes it all down.

Quiet until you need it

New points fade in gently and the view follows the conversation on its own. Nothing asks for a click. When you do want to step in, one click on a node is all it takes.

The room looks at the same thing

Because everyone shares one map, attention gathers around what is still open, instead of being spread across private notes, side chats and memories of what was said.

It listens. It maps. The room decides.

It listens

Wrap-Up follows the conversation as it happens and picks out each point being made: a decision, a question, a concern, a next step.

It draws the map

Each point becomes a node, grouped under the topic the room was on. Topics stack down the page, and a halo marks where the meeting is right now.

The room responds

Anyone can click a node to raise it, lower it, bring it up for discussion, or ignore it. Everyone sees every change at the same time.

AI suggestions come in three kinds

Everything the room needs to finish the meeting it started.

A live map, not a transcript

Points are grouped by topic as they come up. You can take in a whole meeting at a glance instead of scrolling through a wall of text.

Settled vs. open, at a glance

Green is done and orange still needs work. When a topic is fully settled, its group dims and gets a green tick, so it’s obvious what’s left.

Time on every topic

Each group shows how long it has held the floor, so the room can tell when it’s time to move on.

One shared picture

Importance, ignored items, the point under discussion and every note are shared live, so the whole room is literally on the same page.

Notes pinned to topics

Add context, a reminder or a parking-lot item right where it belongs. It stays attached to the topic it’s about.

AI that suggests, never decides

Wrap-Up can flag a question nobody asked or a check worth making. Suggestions are clearly marked, and the room decides whether to take them up.

Ignored, never lost

Ignored items move to the edge of their group instead of disappearing. One click on Revisit brings them back if they turn out to matter.

One thing at a time

Bring any node up for discussion and it’s highlighted for everyone. Mark it done when the room is finished and move on.

Follows the conversation

The view keeps up with the live topic. Scroll back to reread something and it waits for you before following again.

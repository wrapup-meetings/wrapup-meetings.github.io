With WrapUp

Leave with clarity

  • Know where things stand: Keep track of decisions, action items, and questions that still need discussion.
  • Understand what’s next: Leave with a shared understanding of decisions made, action items and responsibilities, and questions that still need to be addressed.

Keep everyone involved

  • Easy to follow: See what is being discussed through a live, AI-organized visual overview of the conversation.
  • Easy to participate: Add notes, highlight important points, and bring attention to ideas that matter to you.

Augment discussions with AI

  • Get timely suggestions: AI can bring attention to unanswered questions, unclear decisions, and topics worth revisiting.
  • Stay in control: Decide together which suggestions to act on and how to guide the conversation forward.

Designed around people, supported by AI.

WrapUp is designed to strengthen collaboration between people during meetings. Our AI-assisted system organizes the conversation and highlights important points that need attention, making it easier for everyone to follow along, share ideas, and decide what to do next.

Built for better discussions.

WrapUp helps teams follow conversations as they happen, contribute ideas, and notice important points without losing focus on the meeting.
